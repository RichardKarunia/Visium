Visium - Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Visium Technologies, Inc. (“Visium,” “we,” “our,” “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you access or use our 3D imaging hardware, software, cloud services, AI systems, and related platforms (collectively, the “Services”). By using the Services, you consent to this Policy.

Visium designs advanced spatial-imaging systems for photography, documentation, modeling, and authorized environmental monitoring. Our devices utilize a combination of high-precision sensors and AI-driven inference capabilities, including Advanced Reconstructive Capture (ARC), which generates predictions or reconstructions of areas not directly visible to the device.

  • Attempt to bypass, disable, or reverse-engineer device or software protections.
  • Obscure or falsify data in ways that interfere with system operation.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We collect information you voluntarily submit, such as:

  • Account details (name, contact information, payment details).
  • Project files, 3D models, spatial scans, still images, and related metadata.
  • Optional biometric-adjacent data, such as depth-based body outlines or environmental motion signatures, when advanced capture features are enabled.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When using Visium devices or software, the following may be collected automatically:

Device and Operational Data

  • Sensor diagnostics, calibration data, and system performance logs.
  • Geolocation data where permitted by device settings or network configuration.
  • Background system processes necessary to finalize or secure scan data, which may run briefly before or after active scans.

Environmental and Spatial Data

  • Depth maps, point clouds, texture cues, and acoustic signatures required to construct spatial representations.
  • Environmental continuity patterns such as surface consistency, reflectivity, and depth gradients.

1.3 Additional Data for ARC (Advanced Reconstructive Capture)

ARC may collect, generate, or infer the following categories of information to support occlusion reconstruction features:

Occlusion-Related Spatial Metadata

  • Edge contours, silhouettes, and object-blocking indicators.
  • Micro-reflections and light-scattering patterns used to infer hidden structures.

Predictive Spatial Outputs

ARC may generate:

  • AI-inferred reconstructions of partially or fully occluded objects or environments.
  • Probabilistic geometry and textures extrapolated from visible data.
  • Multiple candidate reconstructions used internally for confidence scoring.

Supplemental Environmental Data

ARC may reference:

  • Prior scans of the same environment.
  • Aggregated datasets derived from non-identifiable historical scans.
  • Material and structural libraries derived from de-identified data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

2.1 Provide and Improve the Services

  • Operate hardware and software features.
  • Improve spatial-capture accuracy and imaging reliability.
  • Diagnose performance issues and ensure system integrity.

2.2 Develop and Train AI Systems

Visium uses certain data—including ARC-related data—for:

  • Training, testing, and validating machine-learning models.
  • Refining environmental continuity prediction, object inference, and occlusion reconstruction.
  • Building anonymized datasets used to improve recognition of structural patterns, materials, and lighting behavior.

Where required by law, identifiable user content is excluded from training unless the user provides explicit consent.

2.3 Provide Recommendations and Insights

  • Generate automated suggestions to enhance scan quality.
  • Offer optional security or monitoring-related analytics based on spatial data patterns.

2.4 Legal, Safety, and Compliance Purposes

We may use or preserve data to:

  • Comply with applicable laws or lawful governmental requests.
  • Protect the integrity, security, and proper use of the Services.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, misuse, or unauthorized scanning activities.

3. How We Share Information

3.1 With Service Providers

We share information with vendors who:

  • Support cloud hosting, processing, or analytics
  • Assist with customer support or technical operations
  • Maintain AI-integrated features such as ARC

These entities may process data in various jurisdictions.

3.2 With Business or Integration Partners

For enterprise or platform integrations, we may share:

  • Spatial models
  • Derived analytical outputs
  • ARC-enhanced reconstructions where necessary to deliver requested features

3.3 Legal and Compliance Disclosures

We may disclose information when required to:

  • Respond to legal processes or enforceable governmental requests
  • Protect Visium’s rights, property, or safety
  • Support audits, compliance checks, or regulatory obligations

3.4 With Your Consent

We share information with additional third parties only when you explicitly authorize it.


4. Your Choices

You may:

  • Adjust device or app settings to limit certain types of data collection.
  • Request access to or deletion of certain information, subject to operational or legal constraints.
  • Opt out of nonessential analytics or marketing communications.

Some essential operational data—including anonymized ARC-related fragments—cannot be deleted while your account remains active.


5. Data Security

Visium employs administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No system can guarantee complete security; users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their credentials and ensuring lawful scanning activities.


6. Data Retention

We retain information as long as necessary for operational purposes, regulatory compliance, system integrity, and legitimate business interests.

Certain aggregated or anonymized datasets, including ARC-derived reconstruction fragments, may be retained indefinitely for research and model improvement.


7. Children’s Privacy

The Services are not intended for individuals under 16. Any spatial imagery involving minors must be captured lawfully with appropriate consent.


8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy periodically. Notification will be provided through email or in-platform announcements. Continued use of the Services constitutes acceptance of updated terms.
